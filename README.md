## Problem Title
SIH 25010: Smart Crop Advisory System for Small and Marginal Farmers
## Problem Description
A majority of small and marginal farmers in India rely on traditional knowledge, local shopkeepers, or guesswork for crop selection, pest control, and fertilizer use. They lack access to personalized, real-time advisory services that account for soil type, weather conditions, and crop history. This often leads to poor yield, excessive input costs, and environmental degradation due to overuse of chemicals. Language barriers, low digital literacy, and absence of localized tools further limit their access to modern agri-tech resources.

Impact / Why this problem needs to be solved

Helping small farmers make informed decisions can significantly increase productivity, reduce costs, and improve livelihoods. It also contributes to sustainable farming practices, food security, and environmental conservation. A smart advisory solution can empower farmers with scientific insights in their native language and reduce dependency on unreliable third-party advice.

Expected Outcomes

• A multilingual, AI-based mobile app or chatbot that provides real-time, location-specific crop advisory.
• Soil health recommendations and fertilizer guidance.
• Weather-based alerts and predictive insights.
• Pest/disease detection via image uploads.
• Market price tracking.
• Voice support for low-literate users.
• Feedback and usage data collection for continuous improvement.

Relevant Stakeholders / Beneficiaries

• Small and marginal farmers
• Agricultural extension officers
• Government agriculture departments
• NGOs and cooperatives
• Agri-tech startups

Supporting Data

• 86% of Indian farmers are small or marginal (NABARD Report, 2022).
• Studies show ICT-based advisories can increase crop yield by 20–30%.

## Proposed Solution
1. The Smart Crop Advisory System for small and marginal farmers aims to provide simple, accessible, and data-driven solutions to improve crop productivity and income. The system will deliver crop advisory services through mobile apps, SMS, and voice messages in local languages, ensuring accessibility even to farmers with basic phones. 
2. By integrating soil health data, IoT sensors, and weather forecasts, the system can offer location-specific recommendations on crop selection, sowing, irrigation, and fertilizer use. Real-time alerts on pest and disease outbreaks will help farmers take timely preventive measures, while daily market price updates and guidance on best-selling opportunities will enhance profitability. The platform will also inform farmers about government schemes, subsidies, and crop insurance, ensuring they benefit from available support. Using AI and machine learning, the system will generate personalized farm-specific advisories instead of generic suggestions. Furthermore, it will encourage sustainable practices such as organic farming, efficient irrigation, and balanced fertilizer application to maintain soil health in the long term. 
3. Community-based knowledge sharing will also be promoted, enabling farmers to learn from each other’s experiences. Overall, the solution is designed to be farmer-friendly, affordable, scalable, and focused on improving both productivity and sustainability.

## Technical Approach
1. The technical approach of the Smart Crop Advisory System for small and marginal farmers focuses on integrating modern technologies with farmer-friendly platforms. Data will be collected from multiple sources, including soil sensors, weather stations, satellite imagery, and government databases, to build a reliable foundation for decision-making. Low-cost IoT devices will be deployed in fields to monitor soil moisture, temperature, humidity, and crop conditions in real time. Advanced AI and machine learning models will analyze this data to predict pest and disease outbreaks, recommend suitable crops, and provide optimized schedules for irrigation and fertilizer use. A cloud-based decision support system will ensure scalability, secure data storage, and fast processing for generating timely advisories. 
2. To make the system accessible, a multilingual mobile application will be developed with offline capabilities, supported by SMS and IVR (voice call) services for farmers with limited internet access. Integration of weather forecasting APIs and crop growth models will help deliver accurate advice on sowing, irrigation, and harvesting, while real-time market price updates from local mandis and government portals will guide farmers in making profitable selling decisions. Personalized farmer profiles, containing information on land size, soil type, and cropping history, will allow the system to generate tailored advisories. Additionally, a community-based digital platform will enable peer-to-peer learning and collective problem-solving. 
3. Throughout the system, data privacy and security will be maintained with encryption and secure authentication measures.
## Feasibility and Viability
1. The Smart Crop Advisory System for small and marginal farmers is both feasible and viable, supported by the availability of low-cost IoT devices, cloud platforms, and mobile technologies that can be adapted to rural conditions. Economically, the system can be implemented at an affordable cost through government subsidies, public–private partnerships, and scalable business models that minimize financial burden on farmers. 
2. Operationally, the use of SMS, IVR, and multilingual mobile applications ensures accessibility even for farmers with limited digital literacy, while socially, community-based knowledge sharing and local language support foster trust and adoption. The system also demonstrates strong market viability by integrating real-time price updates and buyer networks, which help farmers make profitable decisions. From an environmental perspective, the platform promotes sustainable practices such as water conservation, balanced fertilizer use, and organic farming methods, thereby protecting soil health for the long term. Its modular, cloud-based design allows easy scalability from village-level pilots to state or national-level deployments. 
3. Furthermore, alignment with government policies on digital agriculture, crop insurance, and farmer welfare enhances institutional support, ensuring long-term sustainability and adoption of the system.
## Impact and Benefits
1. The Smart Crop Advisory System for small and marginal farmers will have a significant impact by improving both agricultural productivity and farmers’ livelihoods. 
2. Through timely advice on sowing, irrigation, fertilizer application, and pest management, farmers can achieve higher yields with better quality produce. By receiving real-time market price updates and guidance on suitable crops, farmers will be able to sell their produce at better rates, thereby increasing income and reducing post-harvest losses. Optimized use of resources such as water, fertilizers, and pesticides will lower production costs while also promoting sustainable and eco-friendly farming practices that preserve soil health. Weather forecasts and disease alerts will help farmers manage risks and minimize crop failures, ensuring greater stability in income. 
3. The system empowers small and marginal farmers by providing them access to the same scientific and data-driven advisory services that larger farmers benefit from, thereby reducing inequality. With multilingual support through SMS, voice calls, and mobile applications, even farmers with limited digital literacy or basic phones can be included in the digital ecosystem. Additionally, the platform fosters community development by encouraging knowledge sharing and collaboration among farmer groups. By aligning with government policies on digital agriculture and farmer welfare, the system contributes to food security, rural development, and long-term sustainability in the agricultural sector.
